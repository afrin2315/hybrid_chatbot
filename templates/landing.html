The core idea: errors aren't symmetric

Missing a genuine crisis is potentially catastrophic. A false crisis alarm just shows a supportive message and a helpline. Those costs are wildly different — so the system decouples the two jobs: a dedicated crisis detector tuned for recall, and a routine classifier tuned for accuracy and speed. Each is measured on its own terms, instead of forcing one model to a single compromise operating point.

How the cascade decides

Every message flows through three stages — and the demo shows you which one answered.

🛡️
Stage 1 · always on

Safety layer

A high-recall crisis detector scores every message. Tuned to catch 95.7% of crises — it can override everything else.

⚡
Stage 2 · the fast path

Fast tier

A calibrated TF-IDF + LinearSVC answers confident cases in ~1 ms. Handles ~58% of traffic at near-zero cost.

🧠
Stage 3 · hard cases only

Accurate tier

Only the ~42% of low-confidence messages reach a fine-tuned DistilBERT — the expensive model, used sparingly.

"Why not just one model?"

Because we measured it. The gated cascade beats each model alone — and is far cheaper than the transformer.
